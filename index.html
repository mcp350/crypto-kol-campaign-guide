Crypto KOL campaigns: vet, price, launch and measure

A working guide for token teams, exchanges and Web3 products booking KOLs on X, Telegram and YouTube. Maintained by 3betmedia, a creator-led growth agency for crypto, iGaming and prediction market brands.

The short version: follower counts are the least useful number in a KOL deck. Check the wallet, check the audience, agree what you are paying for, and decide how you will measure it before the first post goes out.

1. Know which creator role you need

A KOL is booked for reach and authority. A KOC is a real product user whose content carries proof. Mixing the two up is the first budget leak.
